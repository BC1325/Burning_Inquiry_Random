Discard these cards

    How it works

    Play Burning Inquiry, draw your three cards, then lay your whole hand out in a row and number it left to right starting at 1.

    Enter how many cards are in your hand and roll. You get three different card numbers, all at once, so there is no renumbering between discards. Every possible set of three cards is equally likely.

    Rolling for an opponent? Count their cards, roll, and read the three numbers out loud.
